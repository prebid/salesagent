The short answer

The rule the epic exists for is delivered and cannot be violated. _boundary.py is the only writer of the context echo, a context write elsewhere does not compile, identity resolves once, the environment is read once, and serialization happens at named edges. Those are children .1, .2, .3, .6, .9, .12, .13, .24, .28 and .30 — all closed, each verified by a reviewer who re-ran every line.

Both lanes that were in flight are now merged. .16 landed as 9bcb62f66; .7’s credential disclosure landed as a9affe06b. Nothing is uncommitted and nothing is awaiting review.

What is left is not the boundary. Of 27 open children, 8 are buyer-facing spec defects found while doing the work, 7 are defects in the grading machinery, 2 are test-suite reconciliation, and 10 are cleanups or decisions. None of them re-open the rule.

The distance to “all BDD scenarios are properly fixed” is set by .52, not by the failure count. The in-process suite has zero failures and the in-network suite has one. But 18 of 52 feature files are collected by no test module, so 1473 scenarios have never run. A suite cannot be called correct over scenarios it does not execute.

Split by whether the use case names a tool this seller implements: 763 scenarios across 9 files grade shipped tools and are real missing coverage; 710 scenarios across 9 files name no implemented tool and would collect as a ledger rather than as coverage. The second group needs one scope decision, not 710 triages.
